GharSaathi

GharSaathi is a mobile-first prototype designed to make finding reliable domestic help easier and safer in Tier-2 Indian cities.

The initial pilot concept focuses on Meerut and Chandigarh.

Problem

Households in Tier-2 cities often depend on neighbours, relatives, local references, or word of mouth to find domestic help.

This creates several problems:

Difficulty finding reliable workers
Lack of proper background or police verification
Multiple workers required for different household tasks
Limited information about experience and previous work
Difficulty finding temporary replacements when a regular worker is absent
Trust and safety concerns for both households and workers

The problem becomes especially important for households where both partners are working and have limited time for everyday household responsibilities.

Proposed Solution

GharSaathi is a two-sided platform connecting verified households with verified domestic workers.

Households can post their requirements for services such as:

Cooking
Sweeping and mopping
Dish washing
Child care
Elder care
Plant care
Full-day domestic help
Temporary replacement for 2–3 days

Workers can view suitable opportunities based on their preferred location, timing, skills, and expected compensation.

How It Works
Household Journey
Household registers and completes basic verification.
Household posts its domestic-help requirement.
Matching verified workers are notified.
Workers show interest in the requirement.
Household reviews worker profiles, experience, ratings, and verification status.
Household sends a connection request to a preferred worker.
Worker accepts or rejects the request.
If accepted, the household chooses a payment option.
After successful payment, contact options are unlocked.
Household and worker communicate and negotiate final compensation directly.
Trust and Verification

Worker profiles require:

Identity verification
Address verification
Police verification
Work experience information
Previous employer references where available

Households also undergo basic verification using:

Mobile OTP
Government ID verification

Personal contact details remain hidden until both parties agree to connect.

Temporary Replacement

If a regular domestic worker becomes unavailable for 2–3 days, the household can request temporary help.

Workers matching the same:

Location
Timing
Tasks
Short-term availability

can be notified automatically.

Workers can also mark planned absences, allowing GharSaathi to proactively ask associated households whether they need replacement help.

Association-Assisted Onboarding

Not every domestic worker may be comfortable using a smartphone application independently.

GharSaathi therefore includes an Association Dashboard.

Local domestic-worker associations or partner representatives can:

Help workers create profiles
Upload verification documents
Explain suitable job opportunities
Assist workers in responding to requests
Support workers in Hindi, Punjabi, or English

The worker's consent is still required before accepting a household connection.

Two-Sided Ratings

Both households and workers can rate their experience.

Public reviews are allowed while both sides confirm that the working relationship is active.

After the relationship ends, new public reviews are restricted to reduce retaliatory or malicious reviewing. Private feedback can still be submitted to GharSaathi for quality, dispute, and safety monitoring.

Prototype

This repository contains an early mobile-first clickable prototype of GharSaathi.

The prototype demonstrates three user journeys:

Household

Post Requirement → View Matches → Show Interest → Worker Accepts → Select Payment → Unlock Contact

Worker

View Jobs → Show Interest → Accept Connection → Verification → Mark Absence

Association

Manage Workers → View Opportunities → Assist Worker → Onboard New Worker

and

Chandigarh

Prototype Status

This is an academic MVP prototype, created to test:

Problem relevance
User workflow
Trust mechanisms
Marketplace design
Pricing assumptions
Worker onboarding
Household willingness to pay

It is not currently a production application.

All names, contact information, worker profiles, ratings, prices, and household information shown in the prototype are illustrative.
